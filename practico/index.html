Training Through the Data

Welcome! In this document, we are going to explore my Strava training data from the last two years.

We are first interested in which types of sports I tracked, and how much of each of them. For that, we consider both the summed distance and the number of sessions.

Alright, so most distance was done on the bike, but most individual sessions were runs. Let's look at the evolution of the concrete time spent doing each sport.

Even though most kilometers were on the bike, we will continue with only running because there are more data points. The most interesting question is whether I improved over the course of my running training. For that, let us first consider the distance per run, by the date of the run.

We can see that I practiced running in two distinct blocks one year apart. In 2026, I did three longer distance runs over distances that I did not run in 2025. In addition, in 2026 there is a group of similar distances, representing running the same route.

By distance, there was some improvement. Another interesting metric is the average speed.

Speed is slow to improve, but still we can see a slight trend where my 2026 runs are slightly faster on average than my 2025 runs. In addition, I performed some training high-speed runs in 2026.

I wondered whether a higher elevation gain meant that my average speed was lower. So, let's plot this.

We see that the correlation is only very rough. This might be because I tried harder on the way up, and because for every way up there is also a way down where I can achieve a higher average speed with less effort.
